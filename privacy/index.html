Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains how the VoxDSTAR app for Android (com.jcalado.voxdstar) and this website handle your information. In short: we don't collect any. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting.

Information we collect

None. VoxDSTAR has no server of its own and never sends information to the developer.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your callsign, settings, memories and key map in its private storage on your device. This data is not backed up to the cloud and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The list of recent calls and their recordings are kept in memory only and cleared when the app closes.

What you transmit

VoxDSTAR is an amateur radio app. When you transmit, your callsign and your voice are sent to the reflector you are linked to, where other operators can hear them and the reflector's dashboard may list your callsign. If you turn on position sending, your location is sent too. Amateur radio transmissions are public by nature, and each reflector is run by its own operator.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you transmit or run an echo test. Audio is never stored.
  • Location (optional, off by default): used only while the app is on the air, to show the distance to other stations or, if you choose, to send your position.
  • Network, notifications and running in the background: to stay linked with the screen off.

Third-party services

To work, the app connects to the reflector you choose; to the D-STAR registration server (auth.dstargateway.org) when you link to a REF reflector; to public reflector lists (pistar.uk, xlxapi.rlx.lu) and reflector dashboards when you browse them; and, only if you turn on callsign routing, to the ircDDB/QuadNet network. These services see your IP address and, where needed, your callsign. We do not operate them.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Children

VoxDSTAR is intended for licensed amateur radio operators and is not directed at children under 13.

Your data

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything the app stores by uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: apps@jcalado.com
